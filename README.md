# Identifying Modern Ball-Playing Centre-Backs

This project applies a multivariate statistical scouting framework to identify, profile, and evaluate modern centre-backs and defensive profiles. Moving beyond basic descriptive statistics, this engine applies dimensionality reduction, possession-adjusted metrics, and cosine similarity algorithms to establish robust tactical archetypes and identify high-value market inefficiencies.

The pipeline processes event data to build composite tactical indicators, flagging profiles that fit a high-possession, proactive game model.

---

## Methodology

### 1. Data Filtering & Aggregation
Players were filtered to ensure positional accuracy, statistical significance, and relevance:
* Primary position: Central Defender (DF) and defensive profiles.
* Minimum threshold of 900 minutes played.
* Aggregation of recent seasons to establish a stable, longitudinal performance profile, mitigating variance from small sample sizes.

### 2. Feature Engineering & Contextual Metrics
Several advanced tactical indicators per 90 minutes were constructed from raw event data to reflect the modern demands of the position:
* **Possession-Adjusted Defending (PAdj):** To quantify true defensive output, team possession was mathematically estimated using relative league pass volume. Defensive actions (tackles and interceptions) were then adjusted based on opponent possession. This isolates genuine defensive intensity per opportunity, eliminating the inherent bias of raw counting stats where defenders in low-possession teams artificially accumulate more actions.
* **Expected Threat (xT) Proxy:** A heuristic model quantifying the territorial and offensive value generated by a defender's passing and carrying decisions.
* **Ball Progression:** A weighted combination of progressive passes and progressive carries.
* **Passing Security:** Overall pass completion efficiency, acting as the baseline for build-up reliability.
* **Composite Scouting Score:** An aggregated metric balancing progression, defensive output, passing security, and an age modifier to highlight recruitment value.

### 3. Standardisation
All engineered variables were standardised using z-score scaling prior to any multivariate analysis. This ensures equal weighting across different statistical scales (e.g., passing percentages vs. absolute defensive actions).

### 4. K-Means Clustering & PCA Projection
Players were segmented into distinct tactical roles based on their multivariate profile. The optimal number of clusters (k=4) was strictly validated and selected based on Within-Cluster Sum of Square (WSS) and silhouette analysis optimisation. 
Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was subsequently used to project these multidimensional profiles into a 2D visual space, facilitating the interpretation of latent tactical structures.

### 5. Player Similarity Engine
A non-parametric recommendation system was built using Cosine Similarity. By evaluating the standardised metrics in a multidimensional space, the engine identifies statistical "clones" of elite targets, providing objective, data-driven alternatives for recruitment and succession planning.

## Key Findings

The integration of contextual metrics yielded highly actionable tactical insights:

1. **Offensive Threat (xT) Generation:** The Expected Threat proxy successfully identifies elite ball-playing defenders acting as deep-lying playmakers. **Oleksandr Zinchenko** leads the continent in generating threat from deep areas with an exceptional 8.01 xT proxy, closely followed by highly offensive profiles like Achraf Hakimi (7.03) and Joshua Kimmich (6.60).
2. **The PAdj Reality:** Adjusting for relative possession drastically alters the defensive landscape. The model correctly identifies pure destroyers like **Alidu Seidu** (4.14) and **Mats Wieffer** (4.13), while also rewarding high-volume defenders in dominant teams, such as **Eduardo Camavinga** (3.95).
3. **Tactical Archetypes:** The clustering algorithm identified four distinct roles. Notably, **Cluster 2 (Elite Progressive - 188 players)** captures primary build-up directors who average 5.6 progressive passes per 90, while **Cluster 3 (Destroyers - 173 players)** isolates reactive, high-volume defenders who display significantly lower passing security (78%).
4. **Market Inefficiencies & U-24 Targets:** By plotting the composite scouting score against player age, the framework isolates young talent overperforming their expected tactical output. Teenagers like **Soungoutou Magassa** (19 years old, 1.78 score) and **João Neves** (19 years old, 1.73 score) emerge as statistical anomalies, displaying elite progression and defensive metrics matching those of prime-age veterans.
5. **Automated Succession Planning:** The similarity engine proved capable of finding exact statistical matches for targeted recruitment. For example, querying the matrix for specific profiles yielded highly accurate alternatives, identifying players like **Juan David Cabal** as a 94.2% match for the required tactical role.
